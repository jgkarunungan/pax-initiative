PRUDENCE IN PUBLIC DECISION-MAKING

Senior High School Position Paper Assessment Rubric
Topic: Pax Silica Initiative and the Philippines
Fundamental Assessment & Philosophical Principle
The grade must reflect the quality of the student's reasoning, not whether the teacher agrees with the student's position. A student may support or oppose Philippine participation in the Pax Silica Initiative and still receive the highest score. Evaluate sound judgment, evidence usage, foresight, discernment, and demonstration of practical wisdom (prudence).

Assessment Criteria & Performance Scoring

1. Understanding of the Issue and Context
Max: 15 Points
/ 15
  • Accurate understanding of Pax Silica Initiative objectives and Philippine relevance.
  • Identifies national, economic, technological, diplomatic, and strategic considerations.
  • Distinguishes established facts from assumptions and uncertainties.
2. Quality and Use of Evidence
Max: 20 Points
/ 20
  • Uses credible, relevant, and verifiable sources.
  • Supports claims with appropriate evidence; distinguishes facts, expert analysis, and opinion.
  • Connects evidence directly to arguments presented.
3. Reasoning and Deliberation
Max: 25 Points
/ 25
  • Logical and coherent arguments; examines advantages/disadvantages of Philippine participation.
  • Considers economic, political, technological, security, and national-interest implications.
  • Examines trade-offs, risks, uncertainties, and unintended consequences.
4. Demonstration of Prudence and Related Virtues
Max: 20 Points
/ 20
  • Practical Wisdom: Determines reasonable action in specific situation.
  • Foresight & Discernment: Anticipates long/short term consequences; distinguishes sound arguments.
  • Responsibility, Moderation & Intellectual Humility: Avoids extreme emotional conclusions; recognizes limitations.
5. Objectivity and Engagement with Counterarguments
Max: 10 Points
/ 10
  • Recognizes personal/ideological biases and avoids emotional/misleading rhetoric.
  • Fairly examines opposing positions and acknowledges legitimate uncertainties.
  • Responds to counterarguments using evidence rather than personal preference.
6. Organization, Clarity, and Source Acknowledgment
Max: 10 Points
/ 10
  • Clear, identifiable thesis statement and logical argument structure.
  • Appropriate academic language and meaningful conclusion.
  • Properly acknowledges sources through identifiable citations and references.
